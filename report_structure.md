### 3.5.3 Output Layer

After computing the L1 (absolute) distance between the embeddings generated by the twin networks, the resulting distance vector is passed through a fully connected (linear) layer to produce a raw output value (logit).

During model training, this raw logit is passed directly to the Binary Cross-Entropy with Logits Loss (BCEWithLogitsLoss). This loss function internally applies the sigmoid operation, providing better numerical stability and improving the training process.

During inference (prediction), the raw logit is converted into a probability by explicitly applying the sigmoid activation function. The resulting probability represents the likelihood that the two signatures belong to the same writer. A predefined decision threshold is then used to classify the pair as either a genuine match or a forgery.

**Include a simple inference workflow diagram:**

```
Embedding A          Embedding B
      ↓                    ↓
        L1 Distance
             ↓
    Fully Connected Layer
             ↓
         Raw Logit
             ↓
   Sigmoid (Inference Only)
             ↓
         Probability
             ↓
      Threshold Decision
             ↓
      Genuine / Forged
```
